Camera is off. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.

Keep the entire barcode in view, hold steady, and use good lighting. Supports EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39 and ITF.

Camera help & privacy

Open this HTTPS page directly in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. Avoid an embedded chat browser. If permission is blocked, enable camera access in your browser’s site settings, then retry.

Choose another camera after the first permission request if needed. Stop the camera before switching. The camera stops when you leave this tab.

Camera images and barcode results are processed in your browser. Results stay only in this page’s memory. The decoder library is loaded from jsDelivr; no images or barcode results are uploaded.
